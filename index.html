WELCOME TO NIKI G PILATES

I'm passionate about helping people feel stronger, healthier, and more confident through Pilates. Whether you're new to movement or looking to build consistency, you'll find a welcoming environment to move at your own pace.

With a background as a Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy Assistant at Imperial College Healthcare NHS Trust, I bring a clinical foundation to every class. My focus is simple: helping you release stiffness, improve posture, and move better in everyday life.

Beginner Friendly

All levels Pilates & Strength

West Park, Goring-by-Sea — Wednesday's 6:30pm - 7:20pm (50 mins) — Starts 16th September
Price Options: First Class £8 | Drop-In £12 | 6-Week Block £65

This class is suitable for anyone who wants to get stronger, move better, and reduce posture-related aches and pains from everyday life—whether that's lifting and carrying children, spending hours at a computer, or simply the physical demands of daily life.

As a busy mum myself, I know how easy it is to put yourself last and how difficult it can be to find time to look after your own body. That's why I've created a class that helps you feel stronger, more capable, and more confident, whatever stage of life you're in.

We'll focus on building core strength, stability, and functional strength to support you in everyday life. This beginner-friendly class is perfect if you're looking to move more, feel better, and improve your overall wellbeing. You'll leave feeling stronger, more mobile, and calmer, with a focus on breath and mindful movement throughout.

Whether you're completely new to Pilates or haven't exercised in a while, that's absolutely fine. This class is suitable for all abilities, and I provide personalised attention so you always feel supported, challenged at the right level, and able to progress at your own pace.
