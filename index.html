Niki G Pilates

Feel stronger, move better and feel more confident in your body.

My name is Niki. My classes are made for real bodies, real women and real life. Whether you're struggling with an achy back, stiff hips, tight shoulders or just don't feel as strong or flexible as you used to, these classes will help you feel better, move better and build strength for everyday life!

As a mum to a five-year-old, navigating life in my mid-40s, I've realised how important it is to take care of yourself, look after your body and invest in your long-term health. And most importantly, to make time for YOU!

I run friendly group classes in Goring, combining my experience as a Pilates instructor with strength training to bring you a unique approach to movement. But don't worry, we won't be lifting heavy!

We use light hand weights, combining the core principles of Pilates – control, flow, precision and breath – with low-impact resistance training to help build strength, improve mobility for everyday living, and support longevity and bone health.

It's all about helping to make everyday tasks easier, whether that's picking up the kids, carrying shopping bags or simply getting out of bed in the morning!

Trust me, your back, hips and shoulders will thank you!

My classes are fun, friendly and supportive, no matter where you are on your journey. You're free to move at your own pace. But who doesn't like a challenge?!

Why join?

If you want to have fun, move better and reduce everyday aches and pains, you'll find a warm, welcoming and non-judgemental space here.

Your commitment

You'll begin with an introductory block where I'll guide you through the Pilates fundamentals and introduce you to using light weights. You'll become familiar with the exercises, allowing you to build confidence while gradually progressing the challenge as you get stronger!

Once you've completed the introductory block, you'll be offered a monthly recurring membership to help you stay consistent, stay on track and keep enjoying the benefits of Pilates and strength training.
