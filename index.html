Clinical Expertise & Mindful Movement

Hi, I’m Niki! My journey into Pilates began during my time as a Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy Assistant at Imperial College Hospital London. Working in a clinical environment, I taught both individual and group Pilates sessions as a core part of patient rehabilitation—and saw firsthand how targeted, mindful movement can completely transform recovery, posture, and quality of life.

Driven by this passion, I completed my formal training in the APPI Pilates method—a world-renowned, physiotherapist-designed approach used extensively throughout healthcare settings. To offer a comprehensive movement experience, I went on to specialize further in Reformer Pilates and Pre & Postnatal Pilates, allowing me to safely support clients through every stage of life and physical transition.

By combining a strong clinical background with a supportive, individual-focused teaching style, I bridge the gap between healthcare and everyday fitness. Whether you are recovering from an injury, managing daily stiffness, supporting your body through pregnancy, or looking to build core strength and confidence, I’m here to guide you every step of the way.

Why Pilates Works for Every Body

Pilates is one of the most versatile and inclusive movement practices available. Designed to be adaptable to any fitness level, it meets you exactly where you are—helping you build resilience, restore vitality, and move with grace.

Restore & Protect Your Spine

Hours spent at desks, behind wheels, or staring at screens take a toll on our posture. Pilates gently decompresses the spine, corrects postural slumping, and strengthens the deep stabilizing muscles that keep you standing tall and pain-free.

Clinical Pain Relief & Injury Recovery

Recommended by healthcare professionals and the NHS, Pilates provides a safe environment to heal. By addressing the root cause of muscle tightness and weakness, it effectively resolves issues like back stiffness, frozen shoulder, RSI, and joint wear-and-tear.

Low-Impact, High-Reward Conditioning

Unlike high-intensity workouts that place heavy stress on your joints, Pilates delivers powerful full-body conditioning through controlled, deliberate movements. It tones long, lean muscles while remaining gentle on your body.

A Mental & Physical Reset

By focusing on controlled breathing and precision, Pilates acts as an active form of mindful meditation. It breaks the cycle of daily stress, lowers tension, and leaves you feeling clear, centered, and re-energized.

My Personal Journey

Having personally relied on Pilates to recover from the physical toll of desk work and a major biking shoulder injury, I bring a deeply empathetic and clinical perspective to every class. Whether you are taking your very first step into movement or recovering from an injury, you will find a supportive space tailored to your needs.
