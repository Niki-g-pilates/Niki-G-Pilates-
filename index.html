WELCOME TO NIKI G PILATES

I'm passionate about helping people feel stronger, healthier, and more confident through Pilates. Whether you're new to movement or looking to build consistency, you'll find a welcoming environment to move at your own pace.

With a background as a Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy Assistant at Imperial College Healthcare NHS Trust, I bring a strong understanding of movement to every class to help you feel your best, release stiffness, improve posture, and move better in everyday life.

All Levels Pilates & Strength
West Park Primary School & Goring Methodist Church Hall (50 mins) — From £13

This 50-minute, full-body class is designed to help you feel stronger, move better, and support your body through the real-life demands of everyday life—whether that's lifting and carrying children, spending hours at a computer, or simply keeping up with a busy schedule.

We’ll combine Pilates with functional strength movements like squats, lunges, and core work, occasional use of light weights and small equipment to challenge your whole body and iron out everyday posture aches.
