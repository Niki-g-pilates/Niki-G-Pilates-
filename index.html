6-Week Pilates Strength Block
Wednesdays • 6:30 PM (Starts 11th Nov)
West Park Primary School, Goring-by-Sea

Suitable for beginners and improvers. Ever wanted to try Pilates but don't know where to start? Or perhaps you've practiced before and want to build confidence? This structured 6-week block guides you safely through fundamental strength techniques.

Chair Based Pilates
Thursdays • 9:30 AM
Goring Methodist Church Hall

Chair-based Pilates is a lovely way to keep moving, build strength and improve flexibility and balance, using a combination of seated and supported standing exercises.
