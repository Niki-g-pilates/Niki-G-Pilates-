Strength, Posture & Wellbeing

A Welcoming Space for Every Body & Every Ability

Move well. Feel strong. Feel your best.

I’m passionate about making movement fun, effective and accessible to everyone.

With experience as a Physiotherapy Assistant, teaching rehabilitation exercises to individuals and groups, and as a qualified Pilates instructor, I create fun, engaging and challenging workouts that are safe, effective and adaptable to different abilities.

Whether you’re recovering from injury, rebuilding strength after having a baby or wanting to stay strong and mobile for longer, my workouts offer options for every stage of life, helping you move confidently and feel your best.
