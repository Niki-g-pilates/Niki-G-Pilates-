About me

Hi, I’m Niki!

I started my journey as a Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy Assistant at Imperial College Hospital London, supporting people with movement and exercise.

I’m trained in the APPI Pilates method, a physiotherapist-designed approach that focuses on mindful movement, strength, control and good technique. I’m also Prenatal and Postnatal certified in both Mat and Reformer Pilates, as well as a Level 2 Group Strength Trainer.

I bring this knowledge into every class, creating supportive and approachable sessions for all abilities and stages of life. Whether you’re building strength, returning to exercise after having a baby, becoming stronger during pregnancy or simply looking to move with more confidence, my classes are designed to help you feel strong, capable and supported.

My aim is simple: to help you move better, build strength and feel more confident in your body — without pressure or perfection.
